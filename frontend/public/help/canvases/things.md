# Things

Things are the building blocks of your canvas. They can be anything from a simple text note to a complex AI agent.

## Key Concepts

Every node on the canvas is called a **Thing**. 
- **Text**: Simple markdown notes.
- **Files**: Uploaded PDFs, Images, or Documents.
- **Cards**: Rich interactive elements (like YouTube videos or Web pages).
- **AI Agents**: Smart Things that can process information.

### Connections
Draw lines between Things to establish **Context**. 
- Connecting a Document to an AI Agent allows the Agent to "read" that document.
- Connecting two Agents allows them to pass messages.

## Actions
The title bar of a Thing contains several icons that allow you to:
- **Rename**: Double click the title to rename it.
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon to delete it.
- **Copy**: Click the copy icon to Copy the content of the Thing (or its transcript) to the clipboard.
- **Export**: Click the export icon to export the content of the Thing (or its transcript) to a file.
- **External Link**: Click the external link icon to link the Thing to another Thing on another Canvas.
- **Green brain**: Click the Green Brain icon to display the transcript of the Thing or the result of the analysis.
- **Orange Bot**: Click the Robot icon to display the plan of the analysis.
- **pencil**: Click the pencil icon to edit the content of the Thing.
- **Full Screen**: Click the Full Screen icon to display the content of the Thing in full screen.
- **Iconize**: Click the Iconize icon to reduce the Thing to an icon.

### Creating Things
- **Drag & Drop** files from your computer directly onto the canvas.
- Use the **Toolbar** (bottom center) to add specific tools or agents.

### Organizing
- **Select**: Click to select a Things. Hold `Shift` to select multiple.
- **Link**: Hover over the edge of a node to find the connection dot, then drag to another node.

## Smart Features
- **Smart Analysis**: Right-click a selection of Things and choose a "Template" (like SWOT Analysis) to instantly generate a report.
- **Chat**: Dropping a "New Conversation" on Things lets you ask questions specifically about those selected items.
