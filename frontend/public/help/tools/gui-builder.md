# GUI Tool Builder Guide

The GUI Tool Builder allows you to create interactive forms that your agents can present to users. This is perfect for collecting structured data without a conversational back-and-forth.

## Getting Started
1. **Form Title**: Give your form a user-friendly title.
2. **Submit Label**: Text for the button users will click to submit the form.

## Building Your Form
- **Drag and Drop**: Drag widgets from the left palette onto the central canvas.
- **Rearrange**: Drag fields up and down on the canvas to reorder them.
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon on a field to remove it.

## Configuring Fields
Select a field on the canvas to edit its properties in the right panel:
- **Label**: The question or label shown to the user.
- **Required**: Check if the user must fill this field.
- **Options**: For dropdowns and radio buttons, define the available choices.

## Preview & Save
- **Preview**: See what your form will look like to the end user.
- **Save Form**: Saves your changes. This will also verify that your form is valid.

> [!NOTE]
> When an agent typically asks for multiple pieces of information, a GUI Tool is often a better user experience than a long chat conversation.
